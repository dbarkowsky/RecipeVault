**Portions:** 4
**Time:** 40 mins
## Ingredients
- 2 cups uncooked rice (short grain)
- 2 chicken breasts, diced into 2cm cubes
- 1 yellow onion, sliced
- 1 leek, sliced across. Use the whole leek.
- 2 cups dashi stock. You can make this with dashi powder.
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 3 tbsp. mirin
- 3 tbsp. brown sugar
- 4 eggs
## Steps
1. Begin cooking rice in rice cooker or pot.
2. Cut chicken into cubes and fry in pan until beginning to brown.
3. Add onion and cook until softened.
4. Mix dashi stock, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar into the pan. Reduce heat and simmer until reduced somewhat.
5. Near end of reduction, add the sliced leek.
6. Crack eggs open into the pan. Do not stir. Just cover with a lid or pizza pan for a few minutes until the eggs are your desired consistency. The goal is a creamy interior.
7. Serve rice and top with chicken mixture.
