- **Portions:** 4
- **Time:** 6.5 hours

This can be eaten two ways:
- In tortillas. I'd recommend keeping it thicker, even adding flour if needed. Rice in the tortillas is also a must.
- On rice. This is the original version, but I prefer having a handheld option.
## Ingredients
- 1 lb. chicken breast
- olive oil
- 1 yellow onion
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp. cumin
- 1 tsp. salt
- 16 oz. enchilada sauce
	- I usually just buy a large jar of picante salsa
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- large tortillas (8-10)
- 14 oz. canned black beans
- shredded cheese (cheddar, mozza, or other)
- jalapeno peppers to taste, sliced
- fresh cilantro
## Steps
1. Pre-cook the garlic, onion, cumin, and salt in a pan until softened.
2. Add those ingredients to the slow cooker, then add the sour cream, enchilada sauce, and 1/4 water if it seems too thick. Include the jalapenos if using them.
3. Nestle the chicken breasts into the mixture.
4. Cook on low for 6 hours, then shred the chicken before returning it to the mix.
5. If you're making tortillas, this is the time to assemble them. Add rice, the enchilada mixture, then top with cheese, sour cream, and cilantro.
	1. If you're doing this over rice, it's the same deal without the tortilla.
