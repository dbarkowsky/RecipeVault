**Portions:** 3-4
**Time:** 25 mins
## Ingredients
- Small, soft taco tortillas (ideally flour)
- 2 chicken breasts
- 1/2 cup green onion, diced
- 1/8 cup red wine vinegar
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- 3 cloves garlic, diced
- 1 tbsp. sugar
- oregano
- salt
- pepper
### Other Toppings
This part is up to you, but these are my recommendations:
- lettuce
- cheese
- sour cream
- salsa
## Steps
1. Prep your non-chicken ingredients so you don't dirty your cutting board. Set these aside.
2. Dice your chicken and begin cooking in a large pan over medium-high heat.
3. When halfway cooked, add the remaining ingredients and simmer for about 10 minutes or until the mixture has reduced.
4. Build your tacos!
