- **Portions:** 4
- **Time:** 30 mins
## Ingredients
Measure your rice and water as per the normal ratio you would normally use.
For every cup of rice, use:
- 1/2 a lime's worth of juice
- 1 tsp. salt
- 3 tbsp. chopped cilantro
- 3 tsp. olive oil, divided
## Steps
1. Combine rice, water, salt, and 1/3rd of your olive oil, then cook as desired.
	1. I usually use a rice cooker, but the original recipe expected a pot.
2. Fluff once rice is fully cooked.
3. Toss with cilantro, lime juice, and remaining olive oil until thoroughly mixed.
## Notes
Goes great with [[Slow Cooker Enchiladas]].
