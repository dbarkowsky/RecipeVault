**Portions:** 4
**Time:** 35 mins
## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups uncooked rice. Your choice of type, but we usually use a Japanese short-grain.
- 1 lb. protein (see notes)
- 2 bell peppers, diced (colour your choice, but I like one green, one red)
- 3 stalks of green onion, diced
- small yellow onion, diced
- 4 cloves of garlic, diced
- 1 bunch spinach, diced
- chill flakes
- Thai basil
### Sauce
- 1/3 cup chicken broth
- 2 tbsp. oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp. soy sauce
- 1 tbsp. fish sauce
- 1 tbsp. sugar
## Steps
1. Start your rice cooker with the uncooked rice and the appropriate amount of water.
2. Begin cooking your protein. For tofu, this means just searing the sides of your cubes.
3. As the protein cooks, mix your sauce ingredients.
4. Add your onions, peppers, garlic, and chili flakes to the protein when it's about half-way cooked.
5. Once onions have softened and protein is cooked, add your sauce mixture. 
6. Simmer until sauce has reduced a little, then add your basil and spinach.
7. Remove from heat and stir to incorporate spinach until it wilts. 
8. Serve rice and top with the rest of the dish.
## Notes
- This dish can be made with a variety of protein options. I like ground pork, but diced chicken or cubed tofu works well too.
