- **Portions:** 6
- **Time:** 60 minutes
## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp butter
- several shallots or 1 large yellow onion
- 2 cups of finely chopped mushrooms
- 1 1/2 cups short-grain rice
- 1/2 cup white wine
- vegetable stock
- spinach, chopped
- parmesan
- salt
- pepper
## Steps
1. Melt the butter in a large pot and sauté the onions until softened.
2. Add the mushrooms and wine until cooked for a few minutes.
3. Add the rice, stir to combine, then begin the risotto process, starting with two cups of stock. Continue to add additional stock as needed and cook on a low heat until the rice is fully cooked.
4. Right as the rice is finishing, add the spinach and stir to combine until wilted.
5. Season with an adequate amount of parmesan cheese, salt, and pepper.
