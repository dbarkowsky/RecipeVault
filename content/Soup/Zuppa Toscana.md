- **Portions:** 6
- **Time:** 35 mins
## Ingredients
- bacon (optional)
- 6 cloves garlic
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2-3 russet potatoes
- 1 lb. Italian sausage
	- I prefer links (sliced), but ground works too.
- 5 cups chicken broth
- spinach, chopped
- 1 cup heavy cream
- sun-dried tomatoes, 200g-ish
## Steps
1. If adding bacon, fry that first and set aside. Add later near end of cooking.
2. In a large pot, add your garlic, diced onion, and Italian sausage. Cook the sausage through.
3. Add chicken broth and sun-dried tomatoes. The 5 cups is up to you, if you want more-or-less-concentrated soup.
4. Simmer for as long as you'd like until the flavours from the sausage have spread throughout the broth.
5. Add the chopped spinach and heavy cream, stirring to incorporate.
6. Salt and pepper to taste.
