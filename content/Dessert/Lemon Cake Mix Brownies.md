- **Portions:** 30
- **Time:** 30 mins
## Ingredients
### Brownies
- 1 box Betty Crocker lemon cake mix
- 2 eggs
- 1/3 cup neutral-flavoured oil (like canola)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
### Glaze
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
	- Use less if you want the glaze thicker.
## Steps
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
2. Line an 8" square baking pan with parchment paper or aluminum foil.
3. Mix brownie ingredients with a mixer until well combined.
4. Spread batter evenly into pan. It may feel very stiff, so you'll have to press it down.
5. Bake for 15 minutes or until brownies just begin to set, being careful not to overcook.
6. When done, allow brownies to cool before lifting from pan with parchment paper
7. Mix lemon juice and powdered sugar until smooth.
8. Spread lemon glaze on top of brownies.
## Notes
Keep these in the fridge to help the glaze stay more solid.
