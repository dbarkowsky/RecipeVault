**Portions:** 20 balls
**Time:** 20 mins
## Ingredients
- 1 C butter
- 1/4 C sugar
- 2 C flour
- 1/2 C crushed nuts
## Steps
1. Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy
2. Add flour and nuts
3. Form into balls and depress slightly
4. Bake at 350F for 10 minutes
5. Once baked, remove from baking and roll in icing sugar before cooling
