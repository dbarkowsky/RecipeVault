## Ingredients
- 1 bag popcorn Twists
- 227 grams butter
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup light corn syrup
- 1 tsp baking soda
## Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 250F
2. Place the contents of the bag of popcorn twists into a large roaster pan.
3. Place the butter, sugar and corn syrup into a large saucepan (at least 2 litres). Allow the butter to melt, stirring everything together, and then bring to a low boil.  
4. Boil for two minutes without stirring.  Add the baking soda.  The mixture will foam up and double in size.
5. Allow to sit for a minute and then pour it over the popcorn twists, stirring everything together to coat the popcorn.
6. Roast in the preheated oven for 45 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes.
7. Remove from the oven, pour onto a sheet of waxed paper and allow to cool.  
8. Break apart and enjoy.
